# National Beverage/CPG Angel Ecosystem: Networks, Programs and Named Individual Angels (for Sooz Beverage, pre-seed, Houston TX)

Research date: 2026-10-08. Method: WebSearch only (WebFetch was blocked). Most findings come from search-result snippets of trade press (BevNET, NOSH, Taste Radio, FoodNavigator, Food Business News, New Hope), press releases, podcast pages, and investor-database profiles (Round Funded, NFX Signal, CB Insights, Caplight, PitchBook previews). Investor-database pages are aggregators that are often stale or wrong, so they are flagged wherever a claim rests on them alone. "Last ~3 years" means roughly Oct 2023 to Oct 2026.

Confidence key used in the roster:
- **High**: named beverage/CPG investment(s) or an active CPG-angel leadership role, shown in trade press, a press release or a podcast, with activity in roughly the last 3 years.
- **Medium**: named CPG/beverage deals, but older than 3 years or shown only by an aggregator; or a clear CPG-investor role with few named personal deals.
- **Low**: directory claims only, deals outside CPG/beverage, or no dated CPG deal found.

---

## Key Question 1: Which founders/operators of exited or scaled beverage and CPG brands are verifiable angel investors, and who are the named individual angels (master roster)?

### Takeaway
The best-documented *individual* beverage/CPG angels active in the last ~3 years are not mainly the famous founders of exited brands. They are operator-angels organized into CPG-specific collectives: The Angel Group (Adam Spriggs, Chris Robb, Spencer Slaine, Jamie Borteck), GRT SHT (Rachel Mansfield), Simple Food Ventures (Greer Tessler), Spacestation (Jaxon Stuart) and Morrison Seger (Rogers Healy, Dallas). Among exited-brand founders, Peter Rahal and Jared Smith (RXBAR) and Daniel Lubetzky (KIND, via Shark Tank) have verifiable recent CPG checks. For most other well-known founders (Olipop, Poppi, Health-Ade, Siete, Epic, Recess, Simple Mills), I found **no** verifiable public evidence of personal angel deals, and they should not be presented as angels without further proof.

## Key Question 5: Which of these people and networks are based in or connected to Texas (and Houston/Rice)?

### Takeaway
Texas has a dense CPG investor cluster in Austin (SKU, Springdale, Brand Foundry, Astō/Clayton Christopher, Poppi's Allison Ellsworth, Capital Factory) and an active beverage investor in Dallas (Rogers Healy/Morrison Seger).
