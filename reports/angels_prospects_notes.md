# Sooz angel prospects: notes

Companion to `angels_prospects.csv` (68 individuals, all of them pass `validateCandidates` for the `sooz`/`angel` import). Built only from the four files in `research_notes/Finding CPG angel investors/`. No enrichment, Apollo or other outside lookups were used. Every `url` appears word-for-word in the notes. Only one `linkedin_url` (Ryan Springer) appears in the notes, and the `email` column is blank everywhere: the only emails in the notes are institutional inboxes (onegiantleap@rice.edu, alumni@rice.edu), so they are listed under contact_path instead.

**Before any first touch:** per the compliance notes, Sooz will most likely raise under Rule 506(b). A first message to someone with no prior relationship should ask for advice or a conversation and should not state round terms. Contact one person per firm at a time, for example only one of Spriggs, Robb, Slaine and Cohen.

## Data-quality caveats
- Most facts come from search snippets (WebFetch was blocked during research). Check sizes come mostly from aggregators and sometimes conflict; the CSV shows both figures (Slaine, Goldman).
- 13 summaries are marked "(unverified)", including Borteck's name spelling, Mason Arnold's identity, Max Baumann's investor status, and whether Brian Goldberg (Astō) is the "Brian Goldberg" in Leisure Hydration's round.
- Several roles are old: Kyle Fletcher (2019), Chris Staffel (2021), the Access Opportunity Fund (2021), Eric Elfman (2018), Scott Jensen and the SKU Investor Forum (undated).
- Locations are blank for 24 people whose location the notes don't give. The Angel Group leaders' locations conflict between sources.
- Fund partners with typical checks of about $500K or more (Healy, Spaly, Gilbreath, Graham, Epstein, Goldberg) are capped at 65 or below and tagged `later-stage`.
